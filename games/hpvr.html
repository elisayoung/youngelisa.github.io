Chaos At Hogwarts & Wizards Take Flight are both location-based VR experiences that put you with 4-6 players to experience Hogwarts at the Harry Potter NYC Store. (Images: Warner Bros.)


Overview

Working on this project was a major dream come true for myself as a kid who was a die-hard Harry Potter fan! For these two VR rides, since I joined relatively late in production, I focused on making changes I felt would improve player experience the most before release to public.

Features I targeted:

  • Flight locomotion -- Changed broomstick free-flight collision behavior to slide along walls in order to reduce nausea and maximize player agency when turning the broomstick handle
  • Spell-casting -- Straightened out spell hit gesture and vfx hookup for responsiveness and clear signal to player on whether they cast a spell successfully or targeted an object within range

Additionally I helped with general gameplay polish, multiplayer handling, and haptics implementations for the stage hardware.
